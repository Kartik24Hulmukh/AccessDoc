## Responsibilities and recruitment

All responsibilities start **pending acceptance**: founder/trial coordinator, session facilitator, evidence/privacy custodian, independent accessibility reviewer, security reviewer, qualified legal reviewer, copyright owner, and release owner. One consenting person may accept multiple suitable duties, but not erase independent review. Names must come from actual people accepting the scope; agents must not assign identities.

1. Founder identifies roughly twenty qualified agencies or practitioners from existing relationships or permitted professional/partner directories. Parent/operator handles real issue creation, approved outreach and booking. No bulk scraping of private contact details, implied endorsement, or unauthorized scanning.
2. Screen for current accessibility reporting work, authorized non-sensitive inputs, an imminent or recurring handoff, and availability. Distinguish practitioner/user, report owner/buyer, and recipient/reviewer. Recruit five of each participant role, plus reserves. A dual-role participant occupies one cohort slot only. Prefer multiple organizations; disclose concentration and founder/customer relationships.
3. Include actual assistive-technology users where possible. Offer accessible invitations, consent, documents, breaks, keyboard-only participation, and each person's usual setup. Do not require medical diagnoses or disclosure of disability. At least one independent qualified accessibility practitioner must review generated output; recommend two reviewers for higher-risk mappings/provenance, without claiming they have accepted.
4. Offer a fixed participation honorarium if approved by the operator; it must not depend on favorable feedback, purchase, or approval. Keep budget, actual payments, participant incentives, and any conflicts in the private register.
5. Track invitation drafted/sent, accepted, consented, scheduled, attended, completed, withdrawn, and declined as distinct states. Do not mark booked without the person's acceptance and confirmed time/timezone. Ten invitations or ten issue rows are not ten completed sessions.

Suggested invitation, to be personalized and sent only by an authorized operator:

> We are evaluating an experimental local tool that converts accessibility testing evidence into a reviewable handoff. It is not an audit, certification, or legal guarantee. Could you join a 45-minute session using synthetic evidence and your normal tools? We want failures and corrections as much as successes. No client uploads or credentials are needed. Participation and any recording are optional. If relevant, a later authorized, redacted engagement can test actual recipient use.

## Consent and data handling before every session

Use a controlled-access participant register outside public issues and generated bundles. Give each person an opaque study ID and store the identity/contact linkage separately. Public reporting uses aggregate results or consented pseudonyms; removing a name alone does not anonymize a distinctive agency, date, route, or screenshot. Share a minimally identifying summary and perform re-identification review.

Obtain explicit consent for participation, the task, what observations are collected, who has access, retention, and withdrawal. Obtain separate optional consent for audio/video recording, quotation/publication, recipient contact, and any later real engagement. Declining recording must not prevent participation. A session notes record can suffice. Agree a retention/deletion deadline before collecting anything; delete raw recordings and contact linkage on that schedule except separately justified contractual records. Explain the limits of withdrawal after irreversible aggregate publication.

For controlled sessions use repository synthetic fixtures only. For later engagements, the agency must confirm authority to test/share the export and obtain client permission where necessary. Remove private URLs, query strings, DOM text/selectors, identifiers, screenshots, names and credentials. If safe sanitization is uncertain, stay with synthetic evidence or perform local work without transferring it. Never ask for passwords, API keys or authentication cookies.

Default the optional external-model feature off for the study. Deterministic bundle generation makes no model request, but the server receives full submitted evidence; provider-bound guidance is a distinct boundary. Enabling it requires accurate data-flow disclosure, operator permission and appropriate processing terms, not just consent to the study. Do not include real evidence, capability download URLs, private consent records, or legal advice in public GitHub issues, automation prompts, telemetry, or screenshots.

## Session script and measurement

Prepare one fixed candidate, synthetic fixture, reference brief and expected corrections list; record their hashes. Pilot the script internally without counting internal rehearsals as external participants. Capture full source SHA, artifact digest, mapping/scanner versions, execution mode, OS/browser/assistive technology versions, scope/state and timestamps without secrets.

Run each session in about 45 minutes, with accommodations and actual task duration recorded:

1. Confirm consent and setup (5 minutes). Explain experimental status and recording choice; do not coach the task. Give the same brief and input to each participant.
2. Observe independently (up to 30 minutes). Ask the person to generate, run verification, open HTML, inspect receipt/coverage and PDF limitations, correct the report, and prepare a handoff. Record start/finish per stage, interruptions and every facilitator intervention. A required rescue means assisted completion, even if the final output works. Generation success alone is not full-flow completion.
3. Ask neutral questions (10 minutes): What does this establish? What remains untested? Is this certification? Which wording could be misread as a legal/conformance claim? Would you hand it to a client, and what must change first? Does the untagged PDF block your workflow? Do you want to try a second real, non-sensitive export?
4. Record actual response and correction evidence, not an interpretation chosen to make the gate green. Log inaccessible interactions, confusion, failures, refusals and withdrawals. A prompted discussion of a second export is not a request unless the participant affirmatively asks to proceed.

Predefine a correction as a distinct content/provenance/mapping/claim error requiring a change; do not count spelling edits as equal to a false conformance assertion or split one root error to inflate counts. Preserve severity and before/after evidence. Qualified reviewer independently checks corrections and material omissions. Use all completed correction-review records; publish the denominator and missing records. Do not compute a passing correction median from a favorable subset. Incomplete reviews leave this gate pending.

Report all ten scheduled/attempted slots, withdrawals and replacements transparently. Complete the ten-person cohort before claiming its thresholds. Retests following repairs are labeled retests, never erased failures; substantive candidate/output changes require renewed affected review and a clearly identified final-candidate cohort. Any material provenance error, sensitive-data disclosure, inaccessible essential task, or unsupported conformance assertion blocks external handoff until fixed and reviewed. A certification interpretation fails the zero-misinterpretation gate; do not coach it away and score the original attempt as passing.

## Independent accessibility review

Review the UI and the actual generated HTML, not axe scores alone. With consenting users/reviewers, record keyboard navigation, focus/order/status/error announcements, zoom/reflow/contrast, headings/tables/links, screen-reader reading and locating findings, coverage/unknowns, manual-vs-automated provenance, and a full handoff/recovery task. Record exact browser/OS/AT versions and untested combinations. Check the same observation across receipt, report, OpenACR, VPAT/EAA outputs and optional advisory text. A schema-valid export can still be semantically wrong.

No blanket HTML conformance or PDF accessibility claim follows from this exercise. `docs/HTML_COMPANION.md` records that independent AT testing is absent. This candidate corrects the older broad accessibility wording in `docs/adoption/how-to-read-bundle.md` to distinguish semantic HTML and automated checks from independent usability or conformance. That copy correction is not evidence that human review occurred. Also verify scanner-version statements against the actual candidate rather than historical documentation.

## Real engagement, acceptance and repeat use

After controlled safety/usability gates and appropriate contracting/data approvals, observe five authorized non-sensitive engagements separately from the ten-person study. More than one engagement per person is not another unique participant. Compare matched report assembly plus correction/review work with the agency's existing method; separate testing/remediation time, founder assistance and learning effects. Proposed research success of 50% median time reduction is an experiment target, not a published savings claim.

For each handoff retain reviewed output digest, scope/untested states, reviewer decision and recipient's actual action: received, requested corrections, accepted for a specified purpose, rejected, or no decision. Receipt delivery is not acceptance, and acceptance is not legal sufficiency. Do not promise procurement acceptance or payment conditional on compliance conclusions. Keep contract/payment evidence private; record booked, invoiced, paid, refunded and delivered separately.

Ask repeat participants only when another eligible engagement arises. Log second requested export, second completed handoff and second paid handoff separately, with trigger date and support minutes. No eligible work is not automatically churn. The research target of three paying agencies/two repeats is a proposed business experiment; it is not a substitute for release safety gates. Use a full observation window for later-starting pilots rather than compressing it to a historical October 19 target.

## Approval and release evidence protocol

1. Freeze the final candidate and record its full forty-character Git SHA, release archive/container digest, evidence manifest digest and scope (source-only/local, protected target, or public service). An earlier passing commit is historical evidence only.
2. For hosted scope, operator supplies approved protected-target access and verifies deployed SHA before/after functional smoke, auth/negative contracts, quotas/logging/retention, alert and rollback drills. Record deployment ID, platform, sanitized origin reference, timestamps and evidence digests. An SSO redirect/401 or missing observed SHA means exact-target checks are blocked, not passed. Local-only scope records deployment as not applicable; it cannot imply hosted approval.
3. Share the immutable review packet privately with each actual reviewer: build/CI/security/SBOM receipts, exact-target evidence where relevant, session summaries, material failures/fixes, independent accessibility report, data-flow/public-copy/name-clearance review and unresolved limitations.
4. Each reviewer returns their own dated approval, conditional approval, rejection, or pending response through their authenticated account, verified correspondence, or agreed signing mechanism. Coordinator may index evidence but must not sign on their behalf. Store actual identity, qualifications, role acceptance, scope, exact SHA/digests, reviewed evidence IDs, conditions and conflicts. Publish name/handle only with consent; otherwise refer to a controlled-access identity/assent receipt. A verified identity is still required privately.
5. Named copyright ownership, release responsibility and private security route must be explicitly accepted. Qualified legal reviewer records jurisdiction/operator-specific scope, name clearance and copy/privacy disposition; do not publicly expose privileged advice. Security/accessibility approvals must be independent as required by governance. A release-owner waiver cannot erase a blocked mandatory gate.
6. Release owner reconciles every required disposition. Silence, assignment, checked form fields, AI review and a draft approval are **pending**, not assent. Conditional approval remains blocked until conditions are evidenced closed and reviewer scope confirmed. Verify the final SHA/digest matches every required approval. Changed code, claims, mappings, data flow or deployment invalidates affected approvals; obtain renewed assent for the final candidate.

Approval statement to be completed by the actual reviewer:

> I accept the recorded responsibility and have reviewed the identified source/artifact and evidence for the stated scope. My disposition, date, conditions, limitations and conflicts are recorded in my authenticated response. This approval does not certify audited targets or make a legal/conformance guarantee outside that scope.

## Evidence record and execution checklist

Use `pilot-trial.yml` for a sanitized work record and `release-signoff.yml` for the scoped approval request/response. GitHub forms cannot enforce identity, conditional fields or authenticity: the coordinator must check linked private evidence before closing a gate. Public issues carry opaque IDs, sanitized evidence summaries and digests only.

Minimum controlled-access records:

- Recruitment: participant ID, declared role, eligibility, invitation/acceptance/confirmed booking, incentive/conflict, consent receipt and retention deadline.
- Session: candidate/input/brief hashes, environment, observed steps/times, assistance, correction severity/evidence, comprehension/PDF feedback, second-export request, failure/retest linkage and reviewer decision.
- Engagement: authorization/sanitization receipt, reviewed output digest, matched baseline/support/editing minutes, recipient disposition/evidence, actual payment state and repeat trigger/completion.
- Approval: actual reviewer identity/assent, role/qualifications, timestamp, scope/SHA/digests, deployment receipt or explicit local-only boundary, evidence reviewed, disposition, conditions/closure and conflicts.

Suggested execution sequence, not bookings: coordinator accepts ownership and obtains consent/retention approval; founder recruits and confirms ten distinct participants; facilitator runs the fixed protocol and practitioner subgroup; independent reviewer checks output and unresolved failures; operator validates exact hosted artifact if hosted scope is requested; counsel/security/accessibility reviewers return scoped actual dispositions; release owner checks all gates. Parent/operator owns GitHub issue creation and authentication/workflows. Until those actions occur, all corresponding trials, approvals and bookings remain pending.
